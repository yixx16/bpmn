Pruebas de C10 Registrar el aviso de siniestro
Antes: motor encendido y workers corriendo (node pruebas/demo.mjs workers).
Cada prueba tiene dos variantes. Usa la de PowerShell en PowerShell y la de curl.exe en cmd.


Prueba 1: póliza no vigente. Termina sola, sin tareas humanas.

PowerShell:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message" -ContentType "application/json" -Body '{"messageName":"AvisoReclamacion","businessKey":"DEMO-1","processVariables":{"firma":{"value":"Firma Alfa","type":"String"},"fechaHecho":{"value":"2026-01-15","type":"String"},"inicioVigencia":{"value":"2026-03-01","type":"String"},"finVigencia":{"value":"2027-03-01","type":"String"}}}'

cmd:
curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"messageName\":\"AvisoReclamacion\",\"businessKey\":\"DEMO-1\",\"processVariables\":{\"firma\":{\"value\":\"Firma Alfa\",\"type\":\"String\"},\"fechaHecho\":{\"value\":\"2026-01-15\",\"type\":\"String\"},\"inicioVigencia\":{\"value\":\"2026-03-01\",\"type\":\"String\"},\"finVigencia\":{\"value\":\"2027-03-01\",\"type\":\"String\"}}}"


Prueba 2: cubierto, sin abogado. En el formulario de revisión: monto 50000 y sin exclusión dudosa.

PowerShell:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message" -ContentType "application/json" -Body '{"messageName":"AvisoReclamacion","businessKey":"DEMO-2","processVariables":{"firma":{"value":"Firma Beta","type":"String"},"polizaVigente":{"value":true,"type":"Boolean"},"montoReclamado":{"value":50000,"type":"Double"}}}'

cmd:
curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"messageName\":\"AvisoReclamacion\",\"businessKey\":\"DEMO-2\",\"processVariables\":{\"firma\":{\"value\":\"Firma Beta\",\"type\":\"String\"},\"polizaVigente\":{\"value\":true,\"type\":\"Boolean\"},\"montoReclamado\":{\"value\":50000,\"type\":\"Double\"}}}"

Prueba 5: temporizador de 24 h. Completa la revisión y deja la preverificación abierta.

PowerShell:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message" -ContentType "application/json" -Body '{"messageName":"AvisoReclamacion","businessKey":"DEMO-5","processVariables":{"firma":{"value":"Firma Epsilon","type":"String"},"polizaVigente":{"value":true,"type":"Boolean"},"montoReclamado":{"value":50000,"type":"Double"}}}'

cmd:
curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/message -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"messageName\":\"AvisoReclamacion\",\"businessKey\":\"DEMO-5\",\"processVariables\":{\"firma\":{\"value\":\"Firma Epsilon\",\"type\":\"String\"},\"polizaVigente\":{\"value\":true,\"type\":\"Boolean\"},\"montoReclamado\":{\"value\":50000,\"type\":\"Double\"}}}"

Para disparar el temporizador sin esperar 24 h, con la preverificación abierta:

PowerShell:
$job = (Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/job?processDefinitionKey=Process_C10_RegistrarAvisoSiniestro&timers=true")[0]
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/engine-rest/job/$($job.id)/execute"
